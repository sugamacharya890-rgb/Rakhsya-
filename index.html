Our origin

A name that means protection.

Rakshya began with a simple, painful truth: too many women and girls in Nepal carry their fear in silence.

Rakshya

It started with stories no one wanted to hear.

Conversations with sisters, classmates and mothers kept returning to the same moments — a hand on a crowded micro-bus, a message that wouldn’t stop, a child who went quiet after visiting a relative. Each story ended the same way: “Who would I even tell?”

Silence is not safety.

Stigma, fear of blame and distance from help mean that harassment, abuse and trafficking risks often go unreported in Nepal. We realised safety isn’t only about stopping harm — it’s about making sure nobody feels alone afterward.

“Rakshya” — रक्षा — is the promise of protection. We wanted a name that sounded like a hand held out.

A movement, not just a website.

So we built three things together: a safe, anonymous community where experiences are believed, an instant emergency directory anyone can use in seconds, and personal safety tools designed for real life in Nepal — affordable, discreet and simple.

Our vision.

A Nepal where every woman can walk home without rehearsing an escape, and every child grows up knowing their body, their voice and their boundaries matter. We will not stop until that is ordinary.

Mission pillars

What we stand for, every day.

Three commitments shape every product we design, every story we hold and every partnership we make.

Women’s personal safety

Practical protection for commutes, campuses, workplaces and homes — from discreet SOS tools to know-your-rights guides and self-defence awareness.

  • Personal safety keychains & SOS wearables
  • Street-harassment response guides
  • Legal rights explained in plain Nepali & English

Child protection

Helping parents, teachers and children recognise grooming, abuse and abduction risks early — and connecting families directly to Child Helpline 1098.

Community empowerment

A trauma-informed space where survivors and allies share, listen and organise — because collective voice changes culture faster than any single law.

Privacy first

Anonymous by design. No names, no tracking, every post reviewed by a human moderator.

Education

Workshops and visual guides for schools and communities — consent, boundaries and safe reporting.

Rapid response

Police, women’s, child and mental-health lines — always one tap away, on every page.
