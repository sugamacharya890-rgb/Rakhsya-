Shared experiences

Stories that remind us we’re not alone.

Shared anonymously and approved by our moderators. Details are changed to protect everyone involved.


Street harassmentKathmandu Valley · approved

Every morning on the micro to college, the same man would stand too close. I thought it was my fault for taking that route. When I finally told my friend, she said it happened to her too. We started travelling together — and I stopped feeling like I had to hide.

Workplace misconductPokhara · approved
Content note: unwanted touching at work. Tap to read.

My supervisor made comments for months and then started “accidentally” touching me in the storeroom. I was scared of losing the job my family depends on. Reading stories here gave me courage to call 1145 — they explained my rights and I wasn’t alone anymore.

Child safetyMadhesh Province · approved

A stranger kept offering my niece rides home from school and promised her a job in the city. She told her teacher because they had just learned about “safe and unsafe secrets.” We called 1098 the same day. Please teach your children that some secrets must be told.

Online harassmentOnline · approved

Someone made a fake account with my photos and sent messages to my relatives. I felt so ashamed I couldn’t eat. A friend helped me screenshot everything and report it to the Cyber Bureau. The account is gone. The shame was never mine to carry.

Healing & hopeLalitpur · approved

It has been three years. Some days are still heavy, but I talk to a counsellor now and I sleep through the night. If you are reading this in the dark: it does get lighter. Be patient with yourself. 🌸

Workplace misconductBiratnagar · approved

Our factory had no complaint committee, so nobody spoke up. A few of us women went together to management with a written letter. Now there is a committee and a female officer. Alone we were quiet; together we were heard.

How sharing works

Your story is protected at every step.

  1. You write

    No sign-up. Pick a topic, write as much or as little as you want.

  2. We scrub

    Moderators remove names, places and anything that could identify you or others.

  3. We review

    Every entry is read by a trained human. Urgent risks are flagged to support services.

  4. It’s shared

    Only approved, anonymised stories are posted — to remind others they are not alone.
